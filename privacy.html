PRIVACY POLICY

Your data stays
under your control.

This policy describes the personal Windows utility Magic Drive Backup, maintained by lu1434417495-sys.

Effective October 10, 2026

1. Data accessed

The utility uses Google OAuth and requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. It can access the contents and metadata of files it creates or files explicitly made available to this application, including names, sizes, identifiers, timestamps and checksums when available. It processes selected local files to upload them and downloads authorized files on the owner's instructions. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Photos or the entire contents of Google Drive.

2. Purpose and use

Data is used only to perform the requested file transfers, manage the relevant backup files and folders, and check transfer integrity. The utility does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, sale, or training general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

Magic Drive Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and transfer

File content travels between the owner's computer and Google Drive using encrypted HTTPS connections. Uploaded files are stored in the owner's Google account; downloaded copies are stored where the owner chooses on their computer.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration on the owner's computer. They are not stored in this public website or its repository. Access to the local configuration depends on the computer's account and file permissions. The utility has no separately operated backend that collects backup contents.

4. Sharing

The utility does not sell Google user data or automatically publish uploaded files. Google receives the data necessary to provide Drive storage and OAuth authorization. Sharing permissions remain under the owner's control. Data is not sent to advertising providers or analytics services by this utility.

5. Retention, deletion and revocation

Files remain in Google Drive until the owner changes or deletes them, subject to Google's storage and retention rules. Local files and configuration remain until the owner removes them. A transfer to an existing destination can replace that file; the utility does not promise indefinite version history.

The owner can delete uploaded files using Google Drive and manage the Drive trash, remove local downloads and configuration, and revoke the app's authorization through Google Account connections. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not itself delete files already stored in Drive or on the computer.

6. This information website

This static website does not accept file uploads, collect Google credentials, or initiate Google authorization. We add no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or cookies. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary visitor information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub issues or the developer contact shown on the Google authorization screen. Public issues are visible to others; do not include credentials or private file content.

Changes to the utility's data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date before those practices take effect.
